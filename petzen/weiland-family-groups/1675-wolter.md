## Notes

- The wife is a candidate only: the 1675 marriage of an unreadable-forename Weilandt to Elisabeth Rödemeister of Petzen precedes Wolter's first child by nine months. No baptism names the mother.
- The 1676 burial ('Wilandt's daughter at Petzen') and the 1678 burial ('[?ert] Weilandt', daughter aged 6 weeks) fit Anna Catharina Eleonora and Anna Margaretha; the 1678 age matches the 20 Jan baptism exactly. Both remain probable, not proven.
- Hans Hinrig's 1718 burial age (37 y 6 w) points to a birth in late Dec 1680 — an exact fit with his baptism.
- Johan Tönnis's burial age of 82 implies birth c. 1678, six years before his 1684 baptism; the identification rests on residence and later records, so the age is probably inflated.
- Pre-1700 dates are Julian.
