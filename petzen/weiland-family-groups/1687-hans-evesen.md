## Notes

- One man is assumed across the three marriages because each wife's burial precedes the next marriage and all are 'Hans Weiland of Evesen'. The 1687 groom is 'of Meinsen'.
- Anna Elisabeth's 1699 baptism places Hans 'by the sand pit' in Evesen — the same holding later numbered Evesen No. 21, where Herman Henrich lived and died.
- Herman Henrich (b. c. 1691) and the Hans Heinrich confirmed in 1707 (b. c. 1693) are placed here only by birth estimates and residence; no baptisms for 1688–1698 are in the folder.
