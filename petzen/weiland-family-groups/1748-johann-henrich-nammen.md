## Notes

- The groom's 1759 burial age fits the Nov 1714 baptism, son of [Johan Tönnis](1708-johan-toennis.md).
- The 1784 groom is called Bünte above Weiland, and his father 'Johan Heinrich Weiland', corrected to 'Bünte', of Nammen No. 30. Johann Henrich's 1748 marriage to the widow of Johann Bünte gives a direct reason for the Bünte house name. This is a stronger candidate than the 1745 Bünte marriage suggested in the 1784 page's note.
- If the 1761 burial (aged 59, b. c. 1702) is Catharina Mysing, she would have been about 47 in 1749, which makes her motherhood of the 1749 child unlikely. Either the burial is another woman or the 1749 child is by an unrecorded wife. Unresolved.
- Johann Herman Weiland, also innkeeper at Nammen, married in Feb 1749 — see [1749 family](1749-johann-herman.md). Two Weiland innkeepers in Nammen in 1749 is possible but worth checking against the images.
