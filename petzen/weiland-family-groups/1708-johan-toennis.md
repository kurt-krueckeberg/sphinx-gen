## Notes

- The baptisms call the father 'Johann Weyland'. He is identified with Johan Tönnis because the 1708 marriage says Johan Tönnis had already fathered a child with Anna Ilsabey Brandt, and the Feb 1708 baptism names 'Johann Weyland of Petzen' and Ann Ilsebe Brandes of Nammen.
- The 1711 burial of 'Johan Weÿlandt's son Johan Henrich from Peetzen' gives an age of 13 y 14 w, which does not fit the 1708 child (he would be 3 y 14 w). The reuse of the name in 1714 suggests the first Johann Henrich had died; the age may be misread. Treat as possible.
- Johann Henrich's burial age (44½ in Apr 1759) fits the Nov 1714 baptism.
- Johann Wilhelm (1710) is probably the Johann Friderich Wilhelm Weiland 'of Nammen' who married in 1737.
- Johann Herman Weiland, innkeeper at Nammen, married in 1749 ([1749 family](1749-johann-herman.md)); he may also belong here, but no baptism supports it.
