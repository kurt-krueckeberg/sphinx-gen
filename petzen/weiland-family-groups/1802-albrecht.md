## Notes

- No children are in the folder.
- The groom is probably Albrecht, bap. 1772, son of [Heinrich Ludowig Sr.](1753-heinrich-ludowig-sr.md) — the only Albrecht Weiland in the folder.
- The bride's late husband was a Weiland; her house (No. 21) suggests [Johann Heinrich of Evesen No. 21](1776-johann-heinrich-no21.md), whose third wife was Anna Margaretha Harren of Meinsen No. 1. The village is read as Meinsen in one column and Evesen in the other.
