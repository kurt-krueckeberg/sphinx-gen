## Notes

- The three wives are numbered by the records themselves: the 1764 burial calls Anna Maria the second wife, and the 1770 baptism calls Anna Christina Nerge the third.
- Friderich Wilhelm's 1806 death record names his father 'Friedrich Ludwig Weiland of No. 21 in Evesen' and gives his age as 53 — matching the Jul 1753 baptism.
- Jobst Heinrich's 1808 marriage calls him the 'third legitimate son of the late Johann Heinrich Ludwig Weiland, lodger at No. 21 in Evesen' — matching the three sons of the first marriage in order. His stated age (48) is two years low.
- B-1764a and B-1765a are two pages for what appears to be one baptism (same child, father, sponsors and image 208). The 1767 burial age (3¾) and the Apr 1763 marriage fit Feb 1764. Consider merging the pages.
- The 1758 baptism's godfather is 'Jobst Hen. Weiland, Sander at Berenbusch' — see [1753 Jobst Henrich family](1753-jobst-henrich-jr.md).
- Heinrich Ludowig may be a son of [Herman Henrich Sr.](1720-herman-henrich-sr.md) of No. 21; no baptism supports it.
