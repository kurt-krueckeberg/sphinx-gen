## Notes

- Charlotta Louisa (bap. 5 Apr 1747) and the stillborn daughter (bur. 7 Apr 1747) were born within days of each other, five months after the marriage — very likely twins.
- The 1747 baptism calls the father 'Herman Henrich Weiland junior in Evesen'; the 1747 stillbirth calls him the son of Harm Henrich, master carpenter. See [1720 family](1720-herman-henrich-sr.md).
- The 1753 baptism's sponsor Dorothea Elisabeth is described as the father's half-sister, and the 1756 sponsor is 'Grandfather' — both consistent with Herman Henrich Sr. still living.
- Johann Henrich (1749) is the later Johann Heinrich Weiland of Evesen No. 21 — see [1776 family](1776-johann-heinrich-no21.md).
