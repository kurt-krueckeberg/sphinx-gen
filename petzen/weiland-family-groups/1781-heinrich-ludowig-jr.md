## Notes

- The 1782 baptism names Heinrich Ludowig Weiland, lodger in Petzen, and Dorothea Elisabeth née Steding, four and a half months after the marriage.
- The 1784 baptism names the father 'Johann Heinrich Weiland, lodger in Peezen' with wife Dorothea née Steding. Same mother and status; the father's forename is probably a slip, or he used the full name Johann Heinrich Ludowig as his father did.
- The groom is identified with Henrich Ludowig, bap. 1756, son of [Heinrich Ludowig Sr.](1753-heinrich-ludowig-sr.md), by name and age (25). The 1779 confirmation of a 'Heinrich Ludowig, Peezen' is an alternative candidate (b. c. 1765) with no known parents.
