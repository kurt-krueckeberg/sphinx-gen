## Notes

- Philippina Louise's 1811 marriage names her father as 'the deceased Friedrich Weiland, lodger in Bärenbusch' — the man who died at Bärenbusch No. 5 in 1806.
- The 1806 death record says he had been married 8½ years and left a widow and three children. That implies a second marriage c. 1797 and two more children, none of them in the folder.
- His father is [Heinrich Ludowig Sr.](1753-heinrich-ludowig-sr.md), named in the 1806 record as Friedrich Ludwig Weiland of No. 21 in Evesen.
