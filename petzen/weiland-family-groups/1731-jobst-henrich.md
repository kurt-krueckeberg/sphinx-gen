## Notes

- Rudolphina's 1732 sponsorship as 'wife of Jobst Henrich Weÿlandt from Berenbusch' confirms the couple in Berenbusch.
- The 1737 baptism and 1741 burial say only 'Weiland in Berenbusch'. Jobst Henrich is the only married Weiland known in Berenbusch then; his brother Wilhelm (b. 1715) was unmarried as far as the folder shows. The godfather was 'his brother'.
- The second Henrich Wilhelm (bur. 1745) is named as Jost Henrich's son; his baptism (c. Nov 1742) is not in the folder.
- The 1745 confirmation (an unnamed male of Behrenbusch) fits Jobst Heinrich Jr. at age 13. The 1748 confirmation of Hans Henrich of Berenbusch implies a son born c. 1734–35 whose baptism is missing.
