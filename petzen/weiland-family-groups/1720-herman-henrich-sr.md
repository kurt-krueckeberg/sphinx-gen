## Notes

- Herman Henrich Sr. is called master carpenter in 1745 and 1747. The 1747 burial of a stillborn granddaughter calls Harm Henrich Jr. 'his son', which ties the two generations together.
- Herman Henrich Jr.'s burial age (47 in Jan 1770) fits the 1723 baptism, so he is placed with the first wife.
- Dorothea Elisabeth is described in 1753 as Herman Henrich Jr.'s half-sister, so she is a child of the second marriage; her baptism is not in the folder.
- Heinrich Ludowig is a candidate son only: his family is later tied to Evesen No. 21 (his sons' 1806 and 1808 records), and his burial age gives a birth c. 1727, during the first marriage. No baptism supports it.
- Hans Henrich (1740) is the best fit for the 1767 'youngest son of old Weiland' (age 26 vs. 27 by baptism). The 1753 confirmation of a Hans Henrich of Evesen could be him (age 13) or the soldier's son baptized 1736 ([1730 family](1730-hans-hermann.md)).
- Johann Henrich's burial age (1 y 5 w in Apr 1747) implies a birth c. Mar 1746, about five months after the Oct 1745 baptism. The father (master carpenter of Evesen) matches; the age is the weak point.
- Herman Henrich Sr. may be a son of [Hans Weiland of Evesen](1687-hans-evesen.md); his burial age gives birth c. Feb 1691.
