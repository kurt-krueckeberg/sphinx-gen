## Notes

- The 1808 marriage calls the groom the 'third legitimate son of the late Johann Heinrich Ludwig Weiland, lodger at No. 21 in Evesen' — see [Heinrich Ludowig Sr.](1753-heinrich-ludowig-sr.md). It also says he fathered an illegitimate daughter Philippine with the bride 19 years earlier (c. 1789), still living.
- That daughter is Philippine Louise, who married in 1812 as the illegitimate daughter of Jobst Heinrich Weiland, lodger in Petzen, aged 24. Her baptism is not in the folder.
- The 1784 baptism of Philippine Eleonore names Maria Elisabeth Raulfing as mother and 'Heinrich Weiland, farm servant in Peezen' as father. The same mother makes Jobst Heinrich the likely father, but the forename differs. Possible only.
- The marriage to Sophie Dorothee Möller is not in the folder. The 1789 twins were conceived before it. Her 1806 death record lists a widower, one son and two daughters — matching Johann Heinrich, Christine Eleonore and Eleonore Louise.
