## Notes

- The register's birth-order column ties the series together: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 6th and 7th children. The 4th child (the first son, c. 1812–1814) is not in the folder.
- The mother's name varies: Catharina Margaretha Drinkuth (1804), Catharine Hahnen of Wülpe No. 1 (1805), Catharine Elisabeth née Döpking of Wülpke No. 1 (1808), Anne Catharine née Drinckuth of Wülpke No. 1 (1811–1819). The house (Wülpke No. 1) is constant; the 1805 and 1808 surnames are probably misreadings to check against the images.
- The 1805 baptism calls the father 'Wilhelm Weiland' of Evesen with no house number; the 1815 stillbirth page's introduction names 'Heinrich Wilhelm Weiland', but its table gives Friedrich Wilhelm of No. 20.
- The 1804 marriage page's introduction says '2 December 1808'; its table caption says 2 Dec 1804, and the 1805 '1st child' fits 1804.
- The groom is the 1777 son of [Friedrich Wilhelm of No. 20](1761-friedrich-wilhelm-no20.md), confirmed 1790 as 'of Evesen 20'.
