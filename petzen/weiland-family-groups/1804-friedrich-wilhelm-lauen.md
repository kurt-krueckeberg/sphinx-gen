## Notes

- The groom is identified with Friderich Wilhelm, bap. 1782, son of [Heinrich Ludowig Jr.](1781-heinrich-ludowig-jr.md): that baptism's margin reads 'Married April 22, 1807', the same day and month as this 22 Apr 1804 marriage. One of the years is misread or miswritten.
- The bride had premarital relations with the groom (1804), so a first child c. 1804 is likely; the 1st and 2nd children are not in the folder.
- The 1812 birth order ('4th child, 3rd son') conflicts with the 1809 entry ('3rd child, 3rd daughter') — at most one son could precede Ernst Heinrich. Check the image.
