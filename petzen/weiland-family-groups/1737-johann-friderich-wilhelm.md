## Notes

- The 1738–1744 baptisms say only 'Weiland of Nordholz, at Ostermeier's place'; the 1748 and 1751 baptisms name the father Wilhelm Weiland of Nordholz, and the 1752 burial calls him Johann Friedrich Weiland of Nordholz. All three forms fit the 1737 groom.
- Every confirmation of a Nordholz Weiland from 1751 to 1769 matches a child here at age 12–14.
- The 1784 burial of Johann Heinrich Weilandt of Nordholz carries the marginal note 'baptized 7 Sep 1755, married 24 Apr 1774', which ties the 1755 baptism to the 1774 marriage directly.
- Anna Maria (1748) is identified with the bride of 1769 ('of Nordholz No. 6'); later baptisms call her 'née Weiland from Nordholtz'.
- The father is probably Johann Wilhelm, bap. 1710, son of [Johan Tönnis](1708-johan-toennis.md) — the 1737 groom was 'of Nammen'.
