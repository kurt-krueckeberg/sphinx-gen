## Notes

- The 1730 baptism names both parents seven months before their marriage.
- The 1735 burial calls the father a soldier; the age (6 y 39 w) implies a birth c. Feb 1729, one year before the baptism. Treat the age as approximate.
- The 1736 baptism names only 'Soldier Weiland from Evesen'; the soldier status ties it to Hans Hermann.
- Hans Hermann's baptism (1705) is placed here by name and residence (Berenbusch) — see [1702 family](1702-hans-hinrich.md).
