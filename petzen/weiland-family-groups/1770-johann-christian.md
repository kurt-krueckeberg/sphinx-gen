## Notes

- Every baptism and burial names Johann Christian, a lodger, and (where given) his wife Christina Ilsabei née Steding.
- The 1770 marriage calls Johann Christian the illegitimate son of Wilhelm Weiland of Nordholz — see [1737 family](1737-johann-friderich-wilhelm.md).
- The first Anna Philippina (b. c. Dec 1774) has no baptism in the folder. The reuse of the name in 1781, after her 1779 burial, supports two daughters of that name.
- The 1778 stillbirth burial gives 17 Jul in the translation; the stillbirth page gives 27 Jul.
