## Notes

- Strong family: the father's 1718 burial age matches his 1680 baptism to the week, the 1715 godfather is the maternal grandfather Diderich Sander, and the 1718 son is called posthumous.
- Jobst Henrich's burial age (59 in Oct 1761) matches the Oct 1702 baptism; the first name in that baptism is uncertain ('[Just?]').
- The 1705 baptism names the father simply 'Henrich Weÿlandt from Berenbusch'.
- Johann Friderich has no baptism; he is placed here only because he married 'of Berenbusch' and his burial age gives a birth c. 1706, in the gap between 1705 and 1711.
- Hanß Henrich (1718) may be the Henrich Weiland of Berenbusch who married in 1745 — see [1745 family](1745-henrich.md).
