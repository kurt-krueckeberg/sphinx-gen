## Notes

- Two pages record this one marriage; the second reads the bride's maiden name as '[Rösner?]'.
- The groom may be Hanß Henrich, the posthumous son of [Hans Hinrich of Berenbusch](1702-hans-hinrich.md), who would have been 26.
- The 1747 baptism names only 'Henrich Weiland, lodger in Peetzen'; it is the only Henrich-fathered baptism of the right date but nothing else ties it to this couple.
