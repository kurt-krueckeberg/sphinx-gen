## Notes

- The 1768 burial names Johann Heinrich's mother as Anna Ilsabei née Deerberg, and the 1770 burial of Friderich Wilhelm calls him a son 'by the first marriage' — both confirm the first wife.
- The 1770 baptism explicitly calls Anne Marie née Weiland the father's second wife.
- Sophia Christina (bur. 1772, aged 5) must belong to the first marriage; her baptism (c. 1767) is not in the folder.
- The 1773 baptism names the parents 'Wilhelm Weiland' and 'Anna Catharina née Weiland'. The house (No. 20), the 'née Weiland', and the burial of 'Johann Tönnies Friderich', son of Friederich Wilhelm of No. 20, eight weeks later all point to this couple; the mother's forename is probably a scribal slip.
- The father is not named in any record after Jan 1790, and Anna Maria remarried as a widow in Nov 1790, so he died between Jan and Nov 1790; his burial is not in the folder.
- The father is probably a son of [Johann Friderich](1734-johann-friderich.md) of No. 20; the second wife is a daughter of [Johann Friderich Wilhelm of Nordholz](1737-johann-friderich-wilhelm.md).
