## Notes

- The 1764 baptism names the mother's brother Mönckhoff of Evesen as sponsor, and the 1770 baptism names the mother Ilsa Margarethe née Mönckhoff — both confirm the 1753 bride.
- The 1758 twins are 'Sander's twins from Berenbusch'; Jobst Henrich is called 'Sander at Berenbusch' in Dec 1758, so the twins are placed here.
- The 1754 page's transliteration is headed '1758 Baptism of Jobst Heinrich Weiland', apparently a copy-over; the page title and record give 1754 and Anna Ilse Margaret.
- The 1768 confirmation page is titled Ilse Margaretha, but its translation reads 'Friederich Wilhelm Weiland of Berenbusch'. Check the image.
- The father, Jobst Henrich Sr., died 14 Oct 1761 — see [1731 family](1731-jobst-henrich.md).
