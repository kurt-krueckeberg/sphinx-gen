## Notes

- The 1764 burial of Maria Elisabeth, wife of Johann Friederich Weiland of Evesen No. 20, and his own 1773 burial as a widower of No. 20 link the 1734 couple to that house.
- Sophia Margareta (1738, residence read as 'Evesen?') is matched to the 1748 burial of Sophia Margaretha, daughter of Johann Friedrich Weiland of Evesen. The burial gives her age as 18, which conflicts with a 1738 birth (age 10). One of the two readings may be wrong.
- Friederich Wilhelm of Evesen No. 20 (conf. 1754, so b. c. 1740) is placed here because he later headed the same house; his baptism is not in the folder.
- Diterich Wilhelm (1741): the father is 'Weiland in Evesen, Bodecker's son-in-law', and the godfather is the father's brother in Berenbusch. The brother fits Johann Friderich's Berenbusch origin, but 'Bodecker' does not match Cohmiers. Weak link.
