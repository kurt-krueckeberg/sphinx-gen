## Notes

- Johann Christoph Ludewig's mother, Catharina Elisabeth Bornemann of Petzen No. 5, became the second wife; the 1779 marriage notes premarital relations. He appears as godfather in 1790 as 'Johann Christoph Ludowig Weiland of Evesen No. 21'.
- The 1776 baptism places the father 'at the sand pit'; the 1776 marriage and the 1778 baptism give Evesen No. 21 — the same house as [Herman Henrich Sr.](1720-herman-henrich-sr.md) and [Jr.](1746-herman-henrich-jr.md).
- The 1790 marriage page's facts table gives the groom's house as No. 24; its translation gives No. 21.
- The 1802 bride of Albrecht Weiland is 'widow Anne Marg. Weyland née Vogt' of No. 21. If 'Harren' or 'Vogt' is a misreading, she may be Johann Heinrich's third wife and widow — see [1802 marriage](1802-albrecht.md).
