# 1774 Johann Heinrich Weilandt Marriage

## Record

The April 24, 1774 marriage of Johann Heinrich Weilandt to Ise Philippina
Margaretha Rust from Peezen no. 6 is on {ref}`image 61 <PET-M-1774a>` of the Petzen
"Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen, 1641-1784".

```{figure} images/petzen-band1b-img61-weiland.jpg
:class: image-override
```

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
1.) d. 24t Apr: ist copuliret. Johann Heinrich Weilandt aus
Nordholz mit Ise Anna Philippina Margaretha Rusts Otto Rusts aus Peezen No. 6 ehel. Tochter.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
1.) On April 24, Johann Heinrich Weilandt of Nordholz was married to Ise Anna Philippina Margaretha Rust, the legitimate daughter of Otto Rust of Petzen, No. 6
```
