## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
den 28. 8br [sint] copuliret Johan Tonnis Weyland aus Peezen mitt Annen Jlsabey Brandts aus Nammen, mitt Welcher er schon vorhin ein Fuhr Kind gezeugt
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On October 28, Johan Tonnis Weyland of Peezen was married to Anna Ilsabey Brandt of Nammen, with whom he had already previously fathered an illegitimate child.
```
