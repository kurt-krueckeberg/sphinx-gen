## Transliteration 

```{div} doc-text
d 24t Febr ist hier getauft Ludewig Weilands aus Röcke Einliegers in
Pezerbaum No. 7 mit seiner Ehefrau Anna Christina geb. Nergen erzeugtes
und d 20t huj: abends 4 gebohrnes Töchterlein, welche genennt worden
Johann Sophia von meiner Ehefrau Johanna Sophia geb Rehren.
```

```{note}
One especially interesting part is **“von meiner Ehefrau Johanna Sophia geb.
Rehren”**: meiner Ehefrau means **“my wife.”** The first-person wording therefore
appears to be the pastor himself saying that his wife, Johanna Sophia née
Rehren, was the person after whom—or by whom—the child was named.
```

```{note}
Pezerbaum = Petzer Baum, a small historical Wohnplatz/roadside locality in
the immediate Petzen–Röcke area, probably named for a road barrier
(Schlagbaum), and not an independent village.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On February 24, Ludwig Weiland’s little daughter was baptized here. Ludwig
Weiland, from Röcke, was a lodger at Pezerbaum No. 7. The child was born to
him and his wife, Anna Christine, née Nergen, on the evening of the 20th of
this month at four o’clock. She was named Johanna Sophia by my wife,
Johanna Sophia, née Rehren.
```
