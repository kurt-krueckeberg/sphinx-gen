## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
Dito Weiland von Nordholtz auf Ostermeiers Stäte ein
Söhnl. tauffen laßen Friderich. Wilhelm gettatter
Sanders Sohn von Behrenbusch, sein Bruder von N. und
[einer über den Berg her?]
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On the same date [of May 15], Weiland of Nordholz, at Ostermeier’s place, had a little son
baptized [and named] Friderich Wilhelm. Sponsors: Sander’s son from
Berenbusch, his brother from N., and [one man?] from over the hill
[here/from there?]
```
