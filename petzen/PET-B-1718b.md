## Transliteration

```{note}
**Seel** is an abbreviation for **seelig**—blessed, late,
deceased. 
```

```{div} doc-text
1. den 30t Januarÿ ist Johann Weÿlandt aus Nammen Töchterl[ein]. Catharina Maria getauft Gev[atterinnen] Johann Brandts
aus Nammen [mitlere?] Tochter und Seel Hans Harmans aus Nammen nachgelassenen älteste Tochter
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
1. On the 30th January was the little daughter of Johann Weÿlandt from Nammen, Catharina Maria, baptized. Sponsors: 
the [middle?] daughter of Johann Brandts from Nammen, and the surviving eldest daughter of the late Hans Harman of Nammen. 
```
