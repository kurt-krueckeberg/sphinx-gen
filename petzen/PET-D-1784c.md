## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
35) d 26t [May] ist Sophia Christina Weilandts des Johann Christian Weilandts Einliegers in Peezen Tochter begraben. Sie ist gestorben an den Blattern. Ihres Alters 1 Jahr 14 Wochen.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
35) On 26 May, Sophia Christina Weiland, daughter of Johann Christian Weiland, a lodger in Peezen, was buried. She died of smallpox. She was 1 year and 14 weeks old.
```
