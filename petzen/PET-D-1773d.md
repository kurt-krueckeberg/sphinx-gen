# 1773 Friderich Weiland of Evesen No. 20 Burial

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 25t Maj: ist begraben Friderich Weilandt ein Wittwer aus Evesen No 20. Seine Kranckheit war die Auszehrung. Sein Alter 67 Jahr.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 25 May, Friderich Weilandt, a widower from Evesen No. 20, was buried. His illness was tuberculosis. His age was 67 years.
```
