## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
28) Eodem ist Lowisa Charlotta Weilandt des Johann Heinrich Weilands
Einliegers in Peezen mit seiner Ehefrau Dorotheen geb. Stedingen erzeugte
Tochter getauft. Gevatterin war die Madem: Eleonora Lowisa Gaden aus 
Peezen, deren Stelle Dorothea Möllern aus Peezen vertreten
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
28) On the same day [Oct. 12th], Lowisa Charlotta Weilandt, the daughter of Johann
Heinrich Weiland, a lodger in Peezen, and his wife Dorothea née Steding,
was baptized. The godmother was Mademoiselle Eleonora Lowisa Gaden of
Peezen, in whose place Dorothea Möller of Peezen acted as proxy.
```
