## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 15t Febr. Henrich Ludowig Weiland des Schneiders Ludowig Wieland Sohn zu
Evesen getauft: Gev. Kuhlman zu Evesen.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On February 15{sup}`th`, Henrich Ludowig, the son of Henrich Ludowig tailor
in Evesen, was baptized. Sponsor: Kuhlman at Evesen.
```
