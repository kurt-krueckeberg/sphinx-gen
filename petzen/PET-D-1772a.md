## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 17t Apr: ist begraben Sophia Christina Weilands Friederich Wilhelm Weilands aus Evesen No. 20 Tochter. Ihre Kranckheit war die Auszufrung. Ihr Alter 5 Jahr.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On April 17, Sophia Christina Weiland, daughter of Friederich Wilhelm Weiland of Evesen No. 20, was buried. Her illness was tuberculosis. She was 5 years old.
```
