# 1767 Herman Heinrich Weiland of Evesen No. 21 Burial

## Record

The 20 April 1767 burial of Herman Heinrich Weiland from Evesen No. 21 is on {ref}`image 192 <PET-D-1767a>` of the Petzen *Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*.

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
`+` d 20t Apr: ist hier begraben Hermann Heinrich Weilan. aus Evesen No. 21. Seine Krankheit war das Alter und ein Schlagfluß. Er ist gestorben d 15t ejuso: u[nd] alt worden 76 Jahr 2 Monathe und 2 Wochen.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On April 20, Hermann Heinrich Weiland of Evesen, No. 21, was buried here. His illness was old age and a stroke. He died on April 15 of the same month and had reached the age of 76 years, 2 months, and 2 weeks.
```
