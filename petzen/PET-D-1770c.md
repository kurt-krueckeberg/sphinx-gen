# 1770 Hans Heinrich Weiland of Evesen No. 20 Burial

## Record

The 24 June 1770 burial of Hans Heinrich Weiland from Evesen No. 20 is on {ref}`image 202 <PET-D-1770c>` of the Petzen *Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*.

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 24t Jun: ist hieselbst begraben Hans Heinrich Weiland Friederich Wilhelm Weilands aus Evesen No. 20 ehelicher Sohn. Die Kranckheit waren die Schürckgen. Sein Alter hat er gebracht auf 24 Wochen.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On June 24, Hans Heinrich Weiland, the legitimate son of Friederich Wilhelm Weiland of Evesen No. 20, was buried here. His illness was febrile illness. He had reached the age of 24 weeks
```
