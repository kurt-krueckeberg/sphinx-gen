## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 16 December, the holy baptism was administered to the little daughter
born to Christian Weiland, a lodger in Evesen, and his wife Christina, née
Steding. She was named Christina Margaretha by the wives of young Röbke, of
Ahldag, formerly Busche, of Evesen, and of the master builder Altmeier of
Petzen.
```
