```{div} doc-text
d. 3t Xbr Johann Weÿlands in Peezen
Sohn getaufft genant
Johann Wilhelm, seine gevattern
waren Ernst Wilhelm Ahldag, Johann
Deher, Hinrich Bormann und der
Apotheker gesell von
Minden no. Johann Georg Dahm
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 3 December, Johann Weyland’s son in Petzen was baptized and named Johann
Wilhelm. His godparents were Ernst Wilhelm Ahldag, Johann Deher, Hinrich
Bormann, and the apothecary’s journeyman from Minden, Johann Georg Dahm.
```
