## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
den 8 August. Hans Weyland von Eversen eine Tochter von 3 iahren 6 Wochen begraben laßen Nahmens Anna Maria
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 8 August, Hans Weyland of Eversen had a daughter, aged 3 years and 6 weeks, buried. Her name was Anna Maria.
```
