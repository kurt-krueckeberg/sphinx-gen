# 1779 Johann Heinrich Weiland of sand-pit area of Evesen Burial

The 19 January 1779 burial of Johann Heinrich Weiland from sand-pit area of Evesen is on {ref}`image 222 <PET-D-1779b>` of the Petzen *Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*.

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
3) d 19t Jan. ist beerdigt Johann Heinrich Weilandt des Johann Heinrich Weilands aus Evesen von der Sandkuhle ehelicher Sohn. Er starb an den Blattern alt 2 Jahr 10 Wochen.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 19 January, Johann Heinrich Weilandt was buried, the legitimate son of Johann Heinrich Weiland, from the sand-pit area of Evesen. He died of smallpox, aged 2 years and 10 weeks. 
```
