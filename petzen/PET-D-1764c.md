```{div} doc-text
d 12t Novembr Anna Maria Weilands Heinrich Ludewigs Weilands Einliegers und Schweinhirtens in Röcke 2ten Ehefrau. alt 35 1/2 Jahr
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On November 12, Anna Maria Weiland, the second wife of Heinrich Ludwig Weiland, a lodger and swineherd in Röcke, aged 35½ years.
```
