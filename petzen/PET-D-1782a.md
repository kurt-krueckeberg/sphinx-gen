## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
9) d 11 Mart: ist begraben Anna Philippina Weilands des Johan Christian Weilands Einliegers in Peezen eheliche Tochter. Sie ist gestorben an den Schürckgen. Ihres Alters 22 Wochen.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
9) On 11 March, Anna Philippina Weiland, legitimate daughter of Johan Christian Weiland, a lodger in Peezen, was buried. She died of a febrile illness. She was 22 weeks old.
```
