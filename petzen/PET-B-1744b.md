## Transliteration

```{note}
The **sche** suffix seems to indicate this is the wife of the man whose
surname precedes the **sche**. Thus **Bormanshce** equals Frau **Borman**.
```

```{div} doc-text
Decemb. d. 13. Wieland in Nordholtze auf Ostermeiers Stäte
ein Töchterlein tauffen laßen. [uncertain surname]sche in Nordholtz, Brandsche in N.,
Bormansche in Nordholtz Gevatterin. Catharina Margareta benahmet.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
December 13. Weiland in Nordholz, at Ostermeier’s holding, had a little
daughter baptized. [Wife/woman of the uncertain surname] in Nordholz, Mrs.
Brand in N[ordholz], and Mrs. Borman in Nordholz were the sponsors. The
child was named Catharina Margareta
```
