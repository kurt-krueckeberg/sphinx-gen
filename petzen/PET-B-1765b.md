## Transliteration 

```{div} doc-text
[d 17t Febr.] Johann Heinrich Weilandt Friederich Wilhelm Weiland aus
Evesen ehelicher Sohn getauft. Gevattern waren Rölcke nebst dem Altarman
Hartmann aus Evesen, und dem Zimmermeister Klöpper aus Nordholz.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
[On 17 February] Johann Heinrich Weilandt, the legitimate son of Friederich Wilhelm Weiland
of Evesen, was baptized. The baptismal sponsors were Rölcke, together with
Hartmann, the altar attendant from Evesen, and Klöpper, the master
carpenter from Nordholz.
```
