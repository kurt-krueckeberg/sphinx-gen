## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 5t Jun: ist hier beerdiget worden Johann Heinrich Weilandt Friederich Wilhelm Weilands aus Evesen No. 20 mit seiner Ehefrau Anna Ilsabei geb. Deerbergen erzeugter Sohn. Seine Kranckheit war die Auszehrung, daran er d 2t früh um 9 Uhr gestorben da Er alt worden 3 Jahr 4 Monathe.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On June 5, Johann Heinrich Weilandt was buried here, the son of Friederich Wilhelm Weiland of Evesen No. 20 and his wife Anna Ilsabei, née Deerberg. His illness was consumption (tuberculosis), from which he died on June 2 at 9 o’clock in the morning, having reached the age of 3 years and 4 months.
```
