## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 28t Sept. Chrsitina Maria Weilands des Harm Henrich Weiland zu Evesen
Tocher getauft. Gev Kuhlmans Busche[s?] Deerbergs und Johan Stedings jun.
Frauen.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On September 28{sup}`th`, Christina Maria Weiland, the daughter of Harm
Henrich Weiland of Evesen, was baptized. Sponsors: the wives of Kuhlman,
Busche, Deerberg and Johan Steding junior.
```
