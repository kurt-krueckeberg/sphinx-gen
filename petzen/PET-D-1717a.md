den 12. Novembr ist Hanß Weÿlands aus Evesen Ehefrau Hille Po[w?]cks, so an

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 12 November, Hanß Weyland’s wife from Evesen, Hille Po[w?]ks, who had
died of dysentery, was buried. She was 55 years old.
```
