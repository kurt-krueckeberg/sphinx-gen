## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 21 dito Weiland auf Ostermeiers Stäte von Nordholz ein Töchterl.
tauffen laßen Bormann Fr. in Peetzen Gevatterin
noie Sophia Elisabeth
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 21 February Weiland, at Ostermeier’s place in Nordholz, had a little
daughter baptized. Mrs. Bormann in Petzen was the godmother; name Sophia
Elisabeth.
```
