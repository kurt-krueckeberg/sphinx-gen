## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
9) den 29 April: ist Hans Weÿlandts auß Evesen Ehefrau Anna Catharina Bormans begraben worden. Ihres Alters 56 Jahr.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 29 April, Hans Weÿlandt’s wife from Evesen, Anna Catharina Bormans, was buried. She was 56 years old.
```
