### Transliteration 

```{div} doc-text
4) d 4t Novbr ist copulirt Heinrich Ludawig Weilandt ein Knecht in Evesen
mit der von ihm geschwächten Dorothea Stedingen aus Peezen
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
4. On November 4, Heinrich Ludawig Weilandt, a farmhand in Evesen, was
married to Dorothea Stedingen of Petzen, with whom he had had sexual
relations before marriage.
```
