## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
14) den 6t Maÿ ist Johann Weÿlandts auß Nammen Töchterlein Ilse Margaretha getauft. Gev. die Schillingsche und die Brandtsche.
```

There is a burial record from 4 November 1735 of female child named Elisabeth Margaretha Weilands born 6 years and 39 weeks earlier.
**What this suggests:**
The Margareta Elisabeth Weiland buried on 4 November 1737 was 6 years and 39 weeks old  when she died. "Ilse" is short for Elisabeth. This would place her birth on 4 Feb. 1731, which is within one  month of this baptism record. However, the father's given name in this baptism record is *Johann* whereas the father's given names in the 4 Nov. 1735 burial record are *Hans Harman*.
The burial record explicitly identifies the father as militis (a soldier), and the family therefore *might* have not have been living in the Petzen parish at the time of the child's birth and baptism since soldiers' households moved with postings.

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On May 6, Johann Weÿland of Nammen had his little daughter Ilse Margaretha baptized. Sponsors: Mrs. Schilling and Mrs. Brandt.
```
