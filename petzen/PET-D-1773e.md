## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 12t Novembr ist begraben Johann Tönnies Friderich Weiland, Friederich Wilhelm Weilands aus Evesen No. 20 ehelicher Sohn. Seine Kranckheit waren die Schürckgen. Sein Alter 8 Wochen.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 12 November, Johann Tönnies Friderich Weiland, the legitimate son of Friederich Wilhelm Weiland of Evesen No. 20, was buried. His illness was a febrile illness. His age was 8 weeks.
```
