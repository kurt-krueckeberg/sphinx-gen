# 1725 Family Member of Shepherd Vahlsing Buried

## Record

The 10 August April 1725 of likley a family member of "Schäfer Vahlsing" was bureid. The burial is the
bottom of {ref}`image 141 <PET-D-1725c>` of the Petzen Kirchenbuch *Verzeichnis der
Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*. Neither the name nor the relationship
is given, and the word buried (begraben) is omitted.
