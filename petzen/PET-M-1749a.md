# 1749 Johann herman Weiland of Nammen Marriage

## Record

### Brief explanation

This record contains lengthy remarks by the Petzen pastor concerning a
dispute over parish jurisdiction.

Because Catharina Maria Röbken was still within the prescribed mourning
period after her previous husband's death, the Petzen authorities had
refused to marry her. Inspector Cramer at Lerbeck nevertheless performed
the marriage. The Petzen pastor regarded this as an unauthorized intrusion
into Petzen's parish rights.

The final quoted statement was therefore meant to prevent anyone from later
arguing that, because the marriage had been performed at Lerbeck, the Krug
at Nammen—the inn or tavern holding associated with Johann Herman
Weiland—belonged ecclesiastically to Lerbeck rather than to Petzen.

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 23 febr. Johann Herman Weiland Krüger zu Nammen wurde mit Catharina Maria
Röbken Witwe Schnaken zu Nammen von dem H. Insp: Cramer zu Lerbeck unbefugter
weise copuliret, weil man alhier Königl. Verordnung zufolge der Frau intra
annum luctus keinen Mann geben wollen. Worauf wir sofort bey dem H. Insp.
Cramer gegen diesen Eingriff in hiesige Parochie protestiret, und ausgemacht,
daß in dortiges Kirchen Buch dabey notiret werden solle,

> »es können daraus keine Folgerungen gemacht werden, als gehöre der Krug zu Nammen nach Lerbek«
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
23 February. Johann Herman Weiland, innkeeper at Nammen, was married to
Catharina Maria Röbken, widow of Schnake at Nammen, by Inspector Cramer at
Lerbeck, without authorization, because here, in accordance with the royal
ordinance, they had refused to give the woman a husband within the year of
mourning. We thereupon immediately protested to Inspector Cramer against
this encroachment upon the jurisdiction of this parish and obtained the
agreement that the following should be entered in the church book there:

> “No conclusions are to be drawn from this as though the inn at Nammen belonged to Lerbeck.”
```
