```{note}
Wilhelm Weiland, the father of the groom, had Johann Christian out of wedlock; Johann Christian is explicitly
called his **unehe[licher] Sohn**. And in 1770 Johann Christian is marrying Christina Ilsabei Stedings,
described as **"die geschwächte"** (weakened), which in this context very likely means she had been
impregnated before the marriage—presumably by Johann Christian.
```

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 11t Mart ist copuliret Johann Christian Weilandt. Wilhelm
Weilands aus Nordholz unehe[lich] Sohn mit der geshwächeten
Christina Ilsabei Stedings aus Berenbush
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On March 11, Johann Christian Weilandt, the illegitimate son of Wilhelm
Weiland of Nordholz, was married to Christina Ilsabei Steding of
Berenbusch, with whom he had had premarital sexual relations (or had
impregnated).
```
