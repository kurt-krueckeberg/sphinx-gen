## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
Maÿ d 17t Weiland in Evesen Bodeckers daselbst Schwieger
Sohn ein Söhnlein tauffen laßen Diterich
Wilhelm benamset von s: Brud[er] in Behrnbusch [??]
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
May 17. Weiland in Evesen, Bodecker’s son-in-law there, had a little son
baptized, named Diterich Wilhelm by his brother in Behrenbusch [allda?].
```
