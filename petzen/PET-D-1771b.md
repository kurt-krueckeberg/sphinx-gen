## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 3t Mart ist beerdigt des Johann Christian Weilands Einliegers in Evesen Tochter Christina Margaretha. Ihre Kranckheit war die Brustkranckheit. Ihr Alter 12 Wochen. 
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On March 3, Christina Margaretha, daughter of Johann Christian Weiland, a lodger in Evesen, was buried. Her illness was pneumonia. She was 12 weeks old.
```
