## Transliteraion

```{div} doc-text
Hans Weilandt, auf der Sant Kuhlen Töchterlein
Anna Elisabeth, getaufft den 9 Maji, gevatter
des Kindes Mutter Schwester Lisabeth.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
Hans Weilandt’s little daughter at the Sandkuhle, Anna Elisabeth, was
baptized on 9 May. The godmother was the child’s mother’s sister, Lisabeth.
```
