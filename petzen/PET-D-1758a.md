# 1758 Jobst Heinrich and Hans Henrich Weiland of Berenbusch Burial

## Record

The 22 October 1758 burial of Jobst Heinrich and Hans Henrich Weiland from Berenbusch is on {ref}`image resume at 181 <PET-D-1758a>` of the Petzen *Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*.

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
22. Oct. Jobst Henrich und Hans Henrich Weiland Sanders Zwillinge aus Bernbusch begr. 14 Tage 16 Tage alt
```

```{note} **Sanders** might be the Hausname while **Weiland** is the Familienname.

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
22 October. Jobst Henrich and Hans Henrich Weiland, Sander’s twins from Berenbusch, were buried, aged 14 days and 16 days.
```
