## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
17) d 23t Octobr ist beerdigt Sophia Eleonora Weilands des Johann Heinrich Weilands aus Evesen No. 21 eheliche Tochter. Sie ist gestorben an den Schürckgen und alt worden 3 Wochen und 2 Tage.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
17) On 23 October, Sophia Eleonora Weiland, legitimate daughter of Johann Heinrich Weiland of Evesen No. 21, was buried. She died of a febrile illness. and was 3 weeks and 2 days old.
```
