## Translation

```{div} doc-text
17) On March 24th, Friderich Wilhelm Weiland, the son of Heinrich Ludowig
Weiland, an Einlieger in Petzen, and his wife Dorothea Elisabeth, née
Steding, was baptized. The godfather was Friederich Weiland, a servant in
Evesen.

**Marginal notes:**
Married April 22, 1807.
Died April 11, 1848.
```
