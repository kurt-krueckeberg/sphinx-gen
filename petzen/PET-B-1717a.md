## Transliteration

```{note}
**Seel** is an abbreviation for **seelig**—blessed, late,
deceased. 
```

```{div} doc-text
1. den 30t Januarÿ ist Johann Weÿlandt aus Nammen Töchterl. Catharina
   Maria getauft Gev. Johann Brandts aus Nammen [mitlere?] Tochter und
   Seel. Hans Hartmans aus Nammen nachgelassene älteste Tochter.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
1. On January 30th, Catharina Maria, the little daughter of Johann Weÿlandt of Nammen,
   was baptized. Sponsors were the [middle?] daughter of Johann Brandt from
   Nammen [and] the eldest surviving daughter of the late Hans Harman of Nammen.
```
