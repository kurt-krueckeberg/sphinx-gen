# 1756 Henrich Ludowig Weiland of Evesen Baptism

## Record

The April 28, 1756, baptism record of Herman Henrich Weiland is found on
{ref}`image 194 <PET-B-1756b>` of the *Verzeichnis der Getauften und Konfirmierten 1641-1784*.

He died after one hour.

## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 28t Apr Herman Henrich Weiland des Herman Henrich Weilands zu Evesen Sohn
getauft. Gev. auus nach einer Stunde gestorben
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On April 28, Herman Henrich Weiland, the son of Herman Henrich Weiland of
Evesen, was baptized. Sponsor: Grandfather. [child] died after one hour.
```
