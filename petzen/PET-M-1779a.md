# 1779 Johann Heinrich Weilandt from Evesen Marriage

## Record

Bornemann from Peezen no. 5 is on {ref}`image 64 <PET-M-1779a>` of the Petzen

## Transliteration

```{note}
The phrase **„der von ihm geschwächten“** literally uses *schwächen* (“to weaken”), but in 18th-century church
records it is a euphemism indicating that the man had **had premarital sexual relations with the woman**. It
can sometimes imply pregnancy, but the wording itself does **not prove that she was pregnant**.
```

```{div} doc-text
6) d 28t Novbr ist Johann Heinrich Weilandt aus Evesen No 21 mit der vom
ihm geschwächten Anna Catharina Elisabeth Bornemanns aus Peezen No. 5
copulirt
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
6) On 28 November, Johann Heinrich Weilandt of Evesen, No. 21, was married
to Anna Catharina Elisabeth Bornemann of Petzen, No. 5, with whom he had
had sexual relations before marriage.
```
