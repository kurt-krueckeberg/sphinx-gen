## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 27t Jan: Friderich Wilhelm Weiland Friederich Wilhelm Weilands aus
Evesen bei der Sandkuhle Sohn. Gevattern. der junge Deierberg, Buschen,
Hartman aus Evesen und der Soldat Rinn aus Petzen.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 27 January, Friderich Wilhelm Weiland, son of Friederich Wilhelm Weiland of Evesen
by the sand pit, was baptized. Sponsors: the young Deierberg, Busche, Hartman of Evesen,
and the soldier Rinn of Petzen.
```
