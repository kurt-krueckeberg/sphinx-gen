# 1703 Anna Maria Vahlsing Baptism

The father's name of Vahlsing does not appear in the record. Instead the father's
identity is given as **das Schefer auf der Höckersau** (the shepherd at the Hökersau),
Schefer apparently a dialectic form of Schäfter (shepherd).

## Record

The baptism of Anna Maria Vahlsing is on {ref}`image 103 <PET-B-1703a>` of the Petzen "Verzeichnis der Getauften
und Konfirmierten 1641-1784".
