## Transliteration and Translation

Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
7 den 20t Januarÿ ist Johann Weÿlandt aus Nammen Töchterl[ein]. Catharina Maria getauft Gev[atterinnen] Johann Brandts
```

## Translation


```{div} doc-text
7 On the @0th January was the little daughter of Johann Weÿlandt from Nammen, Catharina Maria, baptized. Gomothers
The [middle?] daughter of Johann Brandts from Nammen Tochter and the surviving eldest daughter of the late Hans Harman of Namen. 
```
