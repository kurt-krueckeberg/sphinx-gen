## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
Dito [October 21] Johann Krückeberg von Behrenbusch sein Sohn Johann Friedrich aet. 13 [Jahr] & 15 Wochen, gleichfalls an der Ruhr gestorben
```

gleichfals (likewise) refers to the prior burial record, which is more legible and reads:
> d 21t Friderich Engelking von Wülpkeberge sein Sohn Henrich Tönnis æt. 11. 2 monat an der roth ruhr gestorben.
This translates as: 
> On October 21, the son of Friderich Engelking of Wülpke mountains, Heinrich Tönnis, died age 11 years and 2 month of bloody dysentery.
**"rothe ruhr"** or **"rote Ruhr"** translates a "bloody dysentery".   

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
Ditto [October 21] Johann Krückeberg of Behrenbusch, his son Johann Friedrich, age 13 [years] and 15 weeks, likewise died of dysentery
```
