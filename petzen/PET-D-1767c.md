## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
d 2t Aug: Anna Philippina Sophia Weilandts des Heinrich Ludewig Weilandts Einliegers in Pezerbaum eheliche Tochter hieselbst beerdiget worden. Ihre Krankheit waren die Blattern und Schürckgen und alt worden 3 3/4 Jahr Ja
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On August 2, Anna Philippina Sophia Weiland, the legitimate daughter of Heinrich Ludwig Weiland, a lodger in Pezerbaum, was buried here. Her illnesses were smallpox and convulsions, and she was 3¾ years old.
```
