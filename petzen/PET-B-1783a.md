## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
4) d 2t Mart: ist Sophia Christina Weilands des Johan Christian Weilands Einliegers in Peezen
mit seiner Ehefrau Christina Ilsabei geb. Stedingen erzeugte Tochter getafut. Die Gevatterin
war Bornemans Ehefrau Peezen No.  5
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
4) On March 2nd, Sophia Christina Weiland was baptized, the begotton
daughter of Johan Christian Weiland, a lodger in Peezen, and his wife
Christina Ilsabei née Steding. The sponsor was the wife of Borneman at
Peezen No. 5
```
