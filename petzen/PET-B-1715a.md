## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
33. den 27 Decembr ist Hans Henrich Weÿlandt aus Berenbusch
Söhnlein Wilhelm getauft Gevater Großvater Diderich Sander.
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
33. On December 27, Hans Henrich Weÿlandt of Berenbusch had his little son
    Wilhelm baptized. The godfather was the grandfather, Diderich Sander.
```
