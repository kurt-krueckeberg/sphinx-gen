## Transliteration

```{div} doc-text
23) d 19t Septbr ist begraben Anna Philippina Weiland des Johann Christian Weilands Einliegers in Peezen eheliche Tochter. Sie ist an den Schürckgen gestorben. Ihres Alters 4 3/4 Jahr.
```



## Translation

```{div} doc-text
23) On 19 September, Anna Philippina Weiland, legitimate daughter of Johann Christian Weiland, a lodger in Peezen, was buried. She died of a febrile illness. She was 4¾ years old. 
```
