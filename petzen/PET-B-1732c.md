## Transliteration 

```{div} doc-text
den 17 Augusti ist Hermann Henrich Weÿlandts
auß Evesen Töchterlein getaufft und von der Frau
Forst-Schreiberin Gaden Anna Christina genant worden
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On the 17th of August, the daughter of Hermann Henrich Weÿlandt of Evesen was baptized, and was named
Anna Christina by the forest-clerk Gaden's wife.
```
