## Transliteration

```{note}
The symbol "Æt" in manuscripts is an abbreviation of the Latin *aetatis*, meaning **“of the age of”** or
simply **“aged.”**
```

```{div} doc-text
Den 14. d. Octobr. ist Hermann Henrich Weÿlandts auß
Eversen Ehefrau Anna Catharina Piels begraben Æt: 32
```

## Translation

```{div} doc-text
On 14th October the wife of Herman Henrich Weÿlandts from Evesen,
Anna Catharine Piel, was buried, age 32.
```
